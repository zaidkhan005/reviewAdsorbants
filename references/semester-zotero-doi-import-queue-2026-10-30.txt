How to use this queue:
1. In Zotero, search each DOI first to avoid creating duplicates already present in an unexported collection.
2. For missing records, use Zotero's Add Item by Identifier (magic-wand button) and paste the DOI. Metadata only; no PDFs are needed for this step.
3. Preserve the candidate ID in the Zotero item note or tag, e.g. SEM-001, SEM-006. Do not alter Better BibTeX citekeys manually just to match a guessed key.
4. Export the relevant Zotero collection with Better BibTeX to a fresh .bib file.
5. Reconcile the new keys against DOI and update data/semester-bibtex-citation-linkage-audit.csv plus data/semester-main-corpus-crosswalk.csv before inserting any new in-text citation.

Important: the candidates are proposed for selective manuscript use, not automatic inclusion. The evidence rules and source limitations are documented in data/semester-citation-expansion-shortlist.csv. Do not count a DOI in this queue as a cited work.

## 35. Broad-corpus cross-check for overlooked graphene/CIP/TC primary studies — 31 October 2026

The broader repository `data/corpus-evidence.csv` was scanned for evidence rows whose target field identified CIP and/or TC and whose adsorbent field contained graphene, graphene oxide or rGO. Fourteen evidence rows across six candidate records were returned.

### Primary empirical overlaps already in semester register

- **CORP-031 / Zhang & Wu (2022), DOI 10.1002/jeq2.20415:** N-rGO for a six-antibiotic trace mixture including TC. Already mapped to **SEM-038**; use the existing candidate and evidence IDs EV-B10-004–EV-B10-009, with the unresolved feed/volume mass-balance hold intact.
- **CORP-024 / Shaha et al. (2025), DOI 10.1021/acsomega.4c11231:** sulfonated GO for CIP. Already mapped to **SEM-016**; reuse EV-B14-011–EV-B14-015 and preserve the source qe/removal discrepancy.
- **CORP-015 / Li et al. (2025), DOI 10.1016/j.psep.2025.107659:** PGOT@GEL phosphorus-doped GO hydrogel for CIP. Already mapped to **SEM-032**; reuse EV-B15-008–EV-B15-013 and preserve the validation mass-balance and water-matrix label holds.

### Review/context records, not new primary adsorption studies

- CORP-001 / Abd El-Monaem et al. (2022), DOI 10.1016/j.arabjc.2022.103743, is a chitosan-composite review that discusses graphene oxide among several components.
- CORP-018 / Mirizadeh et al. (2025), DOI 10.1016/j.ijbiomac.2025.148399, is a review of chitosan composites and includes GO-containing examples.
- DISC-043 / Ashiq et al. (2021), DOI 10.1016/j.envres.2021.111091, is a critical review of carbon adsorbents and mentions graphene, CNTs and activated carbon.

These reviews can be used for conceptual mapping/citation discovery only; their compiled values are not added as new experimental results.

**Outcome:** No new eligible empirical graphene/CIP/TC study was found in the existing broad master evidence corpus beyond the primary studies already represented in the semester register. No candidate IDs or evidence records were duplicated. This is a repository cross-check, not proof that the entire published literature has been exhaustively searched.

This log records source-level disposition of GAP-015 through GAP-017. Original PDFs from the existing Library and primary corpus were used; no new candidate papers were substituted.

## GAP-015 — Hacıosmanoğlu et al. (2022), natural/modified clay review

- Original uploaded review `1-s2.0-S0301479722009707-main.pdf` was opened and inspected. It identifies a Scopus/ScienceDirect search, review window 2000–2021, 6,208 initial records, 250 after the clay keyword, 90 full-text candidates and 82 included studies; the article attributes these counts to its own review process. These are not the current project corpus counts and should not be restated as 2026 literature counts.
- The review provides a useful taxonomy and synthesis of natural clays, organocation/biopolymer modification, pillared clays, biochar-clay hybrids, regeneration, cost and LCA.
- Its numeric comparisons are secondary and combine very different concentrations, pH, doses and fitted models. Example: review p.4 cites Wang et al. (2010) 330 mg/g CIP at 500–4000 mg/L. We have the original Wang paper and cite it directly for capacity/mechanism.
- SI Tables S1–S6 are referenced but were not in the inspected Library PDF. No quantitative claim requiring those tables is being advanced in the semester manuscript.
- Disposition: use this review for taxonomy and citation discovery only; cite primary sources for all quantitative capacities, conditions and mechanisms. Exclude review counts from our screening denominator.

## GAP-016 — Sompornpailin et al. (2024), granular activated carbon for hospital wastewater

- Original uploaded PDF `1-s2.0-S0301479722009707-main.pdf` was also the clay review; Sompornpailin's original GAC paper `ac 1.pdf` was present in the Library as ref turn117file2 and was read as a full-text source. The source is *Science of the Total Environment* 927 (2024) 172095, DOI 10.1016/j.scitotenv.2024.172095.
- The main paper's abbreviation is “SMZ”; the evidence database retains that string as a source label. I found no verified basis in the inspected main-paper content to silently expand it to SMX or another antibiotic. Preserve “SMZ” until source method/SI explicitly resolves identity.
- Main-text adsorption evidence is at low concentration (100 μg/L, 1 g/L GAC, pH7, 25°C, C-HWW matrix); it reports distribution coefficients Kp in L/g and competitive-mixture effects. These metrics are not Langmuir qmax and must not be pooled into mg/g capacity ranking.
- Main text documents 45-day fixed-bed operation and HT-qPCR, but SI Table S13 contains gene abundance data and was not in the inspected Library bundle. Keep ARG claims qualitative and limited to main-text claims; do not make gene-by-gene abundance assertions without that table.
- E. coli growth response is a specific bioassay, not a general conclusion of sterilization or complete ecotoxicity elimination.
- Disposition: preserve SMZ exactly as printed; use the paper's low-μg/L GAC Kp and matrix-labelled results separately; keep ARG/ARB statements qualified and SI-dependent.

## GAP-017 — Wang et al. (2010), ciprofloxacin–montmorillonite

- Original `1-s2.0-S0304389410009106-main.pdf` (*Journal of Hazardous Materials* 183 (2010) 309–314; DOI 10.1016/j.jhazmat.2010.07.025) was opened from the Library. Methods p.2 specify 0.2 g SAz-1 in 20 mL solution, shaken 150 rpm for 24 h; isotherm initial concentration 500–4000 mg/L; pH-edge test at 3000 mg/L.
- Paper reports 330 mg/g (1.0 mmol/g) with Langmuir R²=0.9997. At C0=3000 mg/L, Ce=80 mg/L; corresponding observed uptake under 0.2g/20mL is (3000−80)×0.02/0.2 = 292 mg/g, distinct from 330 mg/g maximum/fitted capacity.
- Mechanism evidence: total interlayer cation release (mainly Ca²⁺) tracks CIP adsorption with slope ~1; XRD d001 expands 15.04 Å to 17.23 Å near saturation; FTIR adds supporting interactions. This supports cation exchange under the tested conditions.
- The high concentration range is far above typical trace-level wastewater. This is an excellent mechanistic paper but not proof that 330 mg/g is attainable in trace-water treatment. No real water, regeneration, column, cost or LCA studies in this source.
- Disposition: keep the primary study as a high-feed mechanistic natural-clay reference and do not transfer absolute capacity into trace-level comparisons.

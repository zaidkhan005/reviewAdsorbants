## Main synthesis claims the draft supports

1. Mineral identity and matrix chemistry influence clay performance. In the matched DISC-002 test, montmorillonite had higher observed TC uptake and faster equilibration than sepiolite; this is a within-study comparison, not a universal ranking.
2. Clay activation is material-specific. Acid-activated bentonite improved the reported batch response versus the purified parent clay, while the raw/activated Tunisian-clay study did not find a consistent benefit from acid/base activation.
3. Processing and deployment are coupled. MW regeneration, acid/base washing, particle purification, monolith calcination and polymer bead formation add process inputs that are rarely inventoried comprehensively.
4. Immobilization into beads or monoliths can aid recovery and reactor handling but can change active-material fraction and capacity denominator. Do not silently compare per-gram wet/composite beads against dry powders.
5. Reported removal is not equivalent to measured uptake, fitted `q_{max}`, retained capacity ratio, or fixed-bed capacity. The draft labels these separately and avoids a cross-paper “highest capacity” league table.

## High-priority caveats preserved

- **CORP-025:** method says 0.03 g/50 mL (0.6 g/L) but conclusion says 0.03 g/L; reported 500/526 mg/g and fitted Langmuir 558.184 mg/g are not internally reconciled. Keep these headline capacities out of rankings.
- **DISC-018:** extreme narrative qmax (>200,000 mg/g) conflicts with tabular values and observed kinetic uptake; concentration labels conflict; possible acid/alkaline transformation complicates attribution to adsorption.
- **CORP-013:** reported q_exp=277.6 mg/g exceeds the 250 mg/g mass-balance ceiling implied by 500 mg/L × 10 mL / 20 mg beads.
- **CORP-023:** the uploaded full text is a preprint. Only abstract headline capacities were checked against the published paper; the published abstract gives diffusion assignments that reverse the preprint's assignments. Recheck the full published text before citing detailed models.
- **DISC-007:** AC comparator uses a different dose than kaolin/beads; PSO-derived qe=144.307 mg/g conflicts with experimental uptake 68.700 mg/g; reported electricity price unit in the cost estimate is unusual and requires verification.
- **DISC-014:** column cycle-total capacities conflict between table (187.11 mg/g summed) and conclusion (178.11 mg/g).
- **DISC-006:** removal includes catalytic transformation/mineralization; do not count its total removal or fitted qmax as adsorption-only evidence.
- Spiked-water results are explicitly not described as evidence from native trace-contaminated wastewater. A single low-desorption step is not described as destruction or complete regeneration.

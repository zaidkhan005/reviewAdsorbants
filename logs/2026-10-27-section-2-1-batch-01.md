# Section 2.1 raw-biomass draft — Batch 01 (2026-10-27)

## Deliverable

Created `manuscript/SECTION_2_1_RAW_BIOMASS_V1.md` as a source-grounded working draft. The section is framed around untreated and minimally processed residues, then explicitly treats modified sawdust/timber waste as a boundary/bridge rather than calling them untreated biomass.

## Source coverage

- CORP-005 — pine bark, oak ash and uncalcined mussel shell; three sulfonamides; unequal doses and a source concentration mismatch retained as caveats.
- CORP-017 — pine bark, oak ash and mussel shell for amoxicillin, ciprofloxacin and trimethoprim; retention/desorption evidence distinguished from wastewater removal; fitted TMP qmax excluded from ranking because it exceeds the mass-balance ceiling.
- CORP-010 — raw rice husk for levofloxacin; fitted qmax of 2.47 mg/g and dose range up to 120 g/L presented together; high dose is not equated with low cost.
- CORP-019 — untreated barley husks and eggshell with activated-carbon comparator; reported removal percentages are kept separate from qmax/kinetic values, whose dose/mass balance conflicts are flagged.
- CORP-004 — chemically modified sawdust for tetracycline; RSM-predicted 98.40% separated from measured validation max 92.37%; secondary-hospital-wastewater non-detect is not rewritten as zero; 88% is water reclaimed, not antibiotic desorption.
- CORP-006 — modified timber waste for vancomycin; observed removal/uptake separated from the implausible 12,179.5 mg/g fitted qmax; preparation burden and lack of complete cost/LCA are stated.
- CORP-016 — low-concentration trimethoprim uptake on algae, wood chips and pruning biochar; source-table units µg/g retained; fitted isotherm Qm separated from observed uptake, and diclofenac excluded from antibiotic-only synthesis.

37 source-linked evidence records across those seven candidate IDs were reviewed for the section, and 16 in-text DOI links were checked against the canonical screening register. No citation URL mismatches remain.

## Scientific boundaries retained

- No universal material ranking is claimed because antibiotic, concentration, dose, matrix, time and metric differ across papers.
- Observed uptake, percentage removal and fitted qmax remain distinct.
- Raw feedstock and modified/activated material are classified separately; CORP-016 biochar is treated as a cross-stage comparator, not as raw biomass.
- Low desorption under a single CaCl2 release test is not described as multi-cycle regeneration or contaminant destruction.
- Quartile metadata is reported independently of study validity.

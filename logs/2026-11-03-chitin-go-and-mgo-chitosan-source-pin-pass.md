## Individual source pins

### CLM-93 — González et al. (2018), DOI 10.1016/j.carbpol.2018.02.021

- **Key:** `gonzalezContinuousFlowAdsorption2018`; evidence EV-SEM-021.
- **Source:** authors' institutional CONICET repository accepted manuscript; final ScienceDirect publisher preview confirms Sips as best overall and a real-water assessment, but does not expose a corrected packing mass.
- **Pins:** Methods §2.2, §§2.4.1–2.4.2, batch isotherm Results and continuous-flow/real-water sections.
- **Batch use:** 10 mg sorbent in 2 mL CIP, pH6.30, 25°C, 120rpm, C0=4–850mg/L. Sips is the best overall model (ns=0.38±0.09); secondary Langmuir q_m=282±11mg/g for nano-GO and73±3mg/g for Chi:nGO 3:1 are fitted model parameters, not observed qe.
- **Column hold:** accepted manuscript literally states 100g in a 1cm-ID column, 1.5cm bed; bed volume≈1.178cm3, implied density≈84.9g/cm3. This is physically impossible. Quarantine Thomas/Yoon–Nelson q0/τ and dynamic capacity values pending correction or author clarification; do not silently replace 100g with100mg.
- **Matrix boundary:** real-water samples were spiked. No field deployment or corrected column lifetime/cost is verified by the publisher preview.

### CLM-94 — Nazraz et al. (2019), DOI 10.1007/s00604-019-3563-x

- **Key:** `nazrazChitosanbasedSorbentEfficient2019`; evidence EV-SEM-023.
- **Source:** official Springer Nature page, PubMed abstract and publisher-hosted previews of Figs. 1–5; full Methods/tables/captions remain subscription-gated.
- **Source-reported endpoints:** abstract reports Langmuir-fitted q_max=1111mg/g for CIP and1000mg/g for norfloxacin separately, plus PSO kinetics and equilibrium times >120min (CIP) and >150min (NOR).
- **Preview limitations:** publisher Figure5 previews show Langmuir regressions, but without visible captions the analyte-to-panel mapping cannot be confirmed. C0, dose, volume, temperature and condition-linked numerical tables are unavailable, so the finite-feed mass balance cannot be checked.
- **Disposition:** keep CIP and NOR separate; 1111mg/g is fitted q_max, not observed qe. Do not rank it as a condition-matched measured capacity. No accessible regeneration, full process cost or cost-per-treated-volume evidence is established.

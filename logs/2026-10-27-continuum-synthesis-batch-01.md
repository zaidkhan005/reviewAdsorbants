## Interpretation boundaries

- The narrative is a draft synthesis, not a publication-ready manuscript section or a performance ranking.
- The literature is heterogeneous: it combines different antibiotics, concentrations, doses, matrices, units, model-derived capacities and observed outcomes. Do not rank these measurements as though they were obtained under common conditions.
- The presence of a Q1 SJR profile does not establish that the study's reported values are valid or that the journal held the same quartile in the paper's publication year.
- The draft excludes DISC-022's detailed table values because the full text remains deferred until the user supplies the PDF.

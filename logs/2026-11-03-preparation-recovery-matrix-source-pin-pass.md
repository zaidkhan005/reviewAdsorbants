## Individual source pins

### CLM-76 — Yasmin et al. (2024), DOI 10.1016/j.heliyon.2024.e33317

- **Key:** `yasminEfficientRemovalCiprofloxacin2024`; evidence EV-SEM-004.
- **Pins:** primary Heliyon/PMC full text: graphite recovery/cleaning §2.3; GO synthesis §2.4; batch method §2.5; methanol reuse §3.6.9/Fig. 8; Conclusion §4.
- **Preparation burden:** manual spent Zn–C battery carbon-rod recovery/cleaning, HCl:HNO3 pretreatment, improved-Hummers oxidation using H2SO4/H3PO4/KMnO4, H2O2 quench, repeated DI-water/30% HCl/ethanol washing, centrifugation and multiple drying steps.
- **Recovery/reuse:** powder is separated by centrifugation/filtration in batch; scaled solid recovery is not tested. Seven methanol cycles are reported, with 92% CIP removal after cycle 7. This is not a cycle-resolved q_e capacity-retention series.
- **Boundary:** no real-water trial, leaching, product yield, reagent recovery, energy or normalized cost inventory. Waste precursor does not establish low total process cost.

### CLM-77 — Shaha et al. (2025), DOI 10.1021/acsomega.4c11231

- **Key:** `shahaExploringAdsorptionEfficiency2025`; evidence EV-SEM-012 and master EV-B14-011–015.
- **Pins:** ACS Omega source PDF, synthesis/functionalization Methods; Fig. 15 and reuse discussion pp. 40829–40830.
- **Preparation burden:** modified-Hummers oxidation with concentrated acid/oxidant inputs (reported 18 g KMnO4 per 3 g graphite), extensive washing and diethyl-ether processing; yield and solvent recovery are not reported.
- **Recovery/reuse:** DMF + 1 M HCl regeneration, >99.14% release within 240 min, and >95.78% initial capacity retained over five cycles. Capacity retention is distinct from desorption efficiency.
- **Boundary:** synthetic single-solute CIP and cation tests; no real wastewater, solvent recovery, regenerant treatment, quantified energy or process cost.

### CLM-78 — Yao et al. (2017), DOI 10.1038/srep45914

- **Key:** `yao3DAssemblyBased2017`; evidence EV-SEM-015.
- **Pins:** official Nature/Scientific Reports full text; preparation Methods, Figs. 7–10/Tables 1–2, regeneration Fig. 13 and associated Methods.
- **Preparation burden:** CNF/GO fabrication includes stated 1000 W ultrasonic output, freezing below −30 °C for >5 h and lyophilization at −55 °C/80 mTorr for 48 h.
- **Recovery/reuse:** monolithic aerogel is recovered from solution; 5 wt.% NaOH regeneration for 5 h. Over ten cycles TC removal declines from 82.1% to 78.9%; this is removal, not measured q_e capacity retention. Fig. 7 does not give a complete condition vector.
- **Boundary:** no real wastewater or monetized cost/energy/yield inventory; upstream CNF/GO costs are not included.

### CLM-79 — Jahan et al. (2026), DOI 10.1039/D6RA02920E

- **Key:** `jahanFabricationMagneticGraphene2026`; evidence EV-SEM-041.
- **Pins:** official RSC/PMC full text: synthesis/material preparation Methods; magnetic separation, ten-cycle methanol reuse and aqueous test/matrix discussion.
- **Preparation burden:** battery-derived graphite purification (including aqua regia), improved-Hummers GO oxidation, Fe-salt magnetic GO preparation, Co-MOF synthesis with cobalt nitrate/2-methylimidazole in methanol, solvent washing and 60 °C vacuum drying.
- **Recovery/reuse:** magnetic separation is demonstrated; ten methanol cycles show removal declining ~98% to ~91%, not measured capacity retention.
- **Boundary:** tests use ultrapure DI water; no real-water, competing-ion or humic-acid study. Product yield, energy, solvent/reagent recovery and cost per treated volume are absent.

### CLM-80 — Tekle Tegie et al. (2026), DOI 10.4314/bcse.v40i5.15

- **Key:** `tekletegieOptimizationCiprofloxacinAdsorption2026`; evidence EV-SEM-042.
- **Pins:** official AJOL metadata and AJOL-hosted primary PDF; preparation, RSM, pharmaceutical-effluent and reuse sections.
- **Preparation/recovery:** modified-Hummers GO, FeCl3/FeSO4 co-precipitation, zirconium nitrate, NaOH pH adjustment, sonication, heating, washing and vacuum drying; magnetic separation is reported.
- **Reuse:** eight cycles with DI water, acetone and methanol rinses (three rinses each), 30 min shaking and 60 °C drying. Body reports 93.73→85.7%; abstract reports 85.45%.
- **Matrix:** a pharmaceutical-effluent sample changes from 8.37 to 2.01 mg/L CIP, which computes to 76.0% removal, not the 90.75% reported by the source.
- **Boundary:** no product yield, solvent/wash-water balance, energy inventory or normalized monetary cost.

## Metric and matrix safeguards

- Capacity retention, adsorption/removal efficiency and desorption efficiency are different metrics and are not interchangeable.
- Magnetic recovery is an operational handling benefit, not proof of lower lifecycle cost or a molecular adsorption mechanism.
- A waste-derived carbon precursor does not demonstrate low total cost when acid/oxidant treatment, washing, drying, yields and energy are not costed.
- Matrix tiers remain separate: SEM-045 uses ultrapure DI water; SEM-039's cited TC-removal point lacks full test conditions; SEM-050 uses 0.45 µm prefiltered and deliberately spiked municipal wastewater; SEM-046 reports one pharmaceutical-effluent sample with conflicting removal arithmetic.

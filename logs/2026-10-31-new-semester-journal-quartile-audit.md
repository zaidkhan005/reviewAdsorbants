# Semester candidate journal-quartile audit — 31 October 2026

## Scope and method

Audited the journals attached to SEM-045–SEM-051 using the latest ranking edition located in this pass: **2025**. Ranking year is separate from the individual paper's publication year (these seven studies are 2022 or 2026). SJR, JCR and CiteScore are recorded separately; a journal's best quartile is not assumed to apply to every category. Most evidence came from ISSN-matched or university-hosted profiles that reproduce SCImago/Clarivate data, plus official publisher journal-statistics pages where available. These are source-level journal-ranking checks, not article-quality decisions.

## Interpretation controls

1. These findings are based on the 2025 ranking edition and must not be called the article-year (2026) quartile.
2. Keep SCImago/SJR, Clarivate/JCR and Scopus CiteScore quartiles distinct.
3. Category-specific quartiles can differ within one journal; never flatten a category table to a universal quartile.
4. Do not make an inclusion/exclusion decision or remove a candidate solely from its quartile until the university/supervisor's actual ranking requirement and accepted provider are confirmed.
5. No DOI citation keys, manuscript citations, or source-evidence values were altered in this ranking audit.

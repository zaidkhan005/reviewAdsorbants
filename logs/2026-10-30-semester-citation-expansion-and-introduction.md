## Prioritized candidates in the DOI intake queue

### Priority P1 — full-text or author-hosted source evidence

- **SEM-001 — GO/TC (2013):** primary PLOS full text and Table 2 checked. Keep the 298/303/308 K q_m values as reported; the Results prose also lists 310/313 K and the temperature trend conflicts with the endothermic interpretation.
- **SEM-006 — DTPA-functionalized magnetic GO/CIP+TC (2018):** primary main PDF available. The 294.12 mg/g TC and 111.73 mg/g CIP figures are explicitly PSO-fitted qe, not qmax or directly observed qe; SI tables are missing.
- **SEM-007 — MnFe2O4/rGO/TC (2018):** primary PMC full text and PDF. Observed condition-specific uptake and HCl regeneration are usable with exact cycle-count caveat; very large Langmuir q_m is outperformed by Freundlich fit.
- **SEM-012 — magnetic GO/CIP (2023):** official full paper and SI. SI validates 96.39% CIP removal, plus tap/river/lake/wastewater outcomes; dose is 36.7 g/L and sonication energy is unreported.
- **SEM-015 — GO/LDH/CIP (2025):** full text. Use only 84.84% removal at the specified 1 mg/L feed and 10 mg/L dose; capacity/qe values are inconsistent with mass balance.
- **SEM-019 — EDTA-functionalized magnetic GO/TC (2026):** full text. Use 93.23% removal under its stated low-C0 condition; kinetic fitted qe and some fitted capacities conflict with mass balance/model-fit quality.
- **SEM-027 — PAM-functionalized GO/alginate beads/CIP (2022):** open-access full text. Useful for composite preparation and fitted-isotherm discussion; no directly observed qe/removal endpoint was extracted and 55 mL/50 mL tube wording is inconsistent.
- **SEM-042 — chitin/GO/CIP (2018):** author-hosted accepted manuscript plus publisher preview. Batch evidence is usable cautiously; all dynamic column values remain quarantined because 100 g is claimed in a 1.178 cm³ bed.

### Priority P2 — publisher abstract/preview or mirror-limited evidence

- **SEM-043 — GO/CIP (2015):** publisher abstract plus third-party mirror for detailed conditions/table. Use with explicit mirror provenance; Freundlich fits better than the headline Langmuir fit.
- **SEM-009 — GO/CIP in aqueous medium and synthetic urine (2024):** abstract/indexed capacity values require verification of model/metric and conditions; synthetic urine is not real wastewater.
- **SEM-011 — magnetic guar-gum-grafted GO/CIP (2023):** detailed table claims come from a third-party mirror and dose statements differ by a factor of ten; keep numeric values held pending stronger provenance.
- **SEM-025 — magnetic chitosan-grafted GO/CIP (2016):** publisher preview only; q_m is abstract-reported fitted capacity, while approximately 72% refers to retained capacity after four uses.
- **SEM-028 — GO/calcium-alginate fibers/CIP (2013):** publisher abstract/section snippets only; keep the 78.9% removal condition separate from the 39.06 mg/g equilibrium-capacity statement.

These are candidates for selective citation after DOI metadata is reconciled; they are not automatically accepted or included. In particular, P2 records should not be used to create false precision or capacity rankings.

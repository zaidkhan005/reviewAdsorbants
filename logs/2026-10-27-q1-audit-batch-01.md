# Journal quartile audit — Batch 01 (2026-10-27)

## Scope and method

- Audited all **34 unique journal titles** represented by the 62 candidate rows in `data/corpus-screening-register.csv`.
- Added a one-row-per-journal register at `data/journal-quartile-audit.csv` and populated ranking metadata for the candidate rows in the main screening register.
- Used **2025 SCImago/SJR** profiles where a 2025 SJR result was found. Arabian Journal of Chemistry uses a historical 2024 Scopus/SJR result because public source-list mirrors report its Scopus coverage was discontinued after 2024. Iraqi Journal of Agricultural Sciences is flagged as discontinued in a current source-list mirror and is not assigned a current quartile.
- Ranking year means the **year of the ranking data**, not the paper's publication year. SJR and Clarivate/JCR are distinct systems and can return different quartiles for the same journal. Both system and category are retained in the notes.
- Most ranking pages accessed were secondary profiles that display or reproduce SCImago/SJR data; the canonical SCImago page was not independently opened for every journal. This is a structured first pass, not a substitute for direct verification in the ranking system required by the institution.

## Interpretation guardrails

- A journal's current quartile does **not** automatically establish its quartile in the year a candidate paper was published.
- A source labeled Q1 in its best category may be Q2 in another category; the subject category must match the purpose of the assessment.
- Ranking is only one screening field. It does not prove that a paper's methods, antibiotic-specific data, adsorption-only attribution, or quantitative results are valid.
- Keep `DISC-022` deferred until the user supplies its full-text PDF. The quartile record does not change its full-text status.

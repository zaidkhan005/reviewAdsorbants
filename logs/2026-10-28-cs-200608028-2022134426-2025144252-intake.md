## Evidence and limitations

### P01 — Gu et al. (2007)

- Equilibrium dialysis shows maximum TC–ESHA interaction near pH 4.3, but <10% of TC added was sorbed at the maximum (PDF p.5, Fig. 3, §3.2).
- A tenfold NaCl ionic-strength increase (0.01→0.1 M) lowered sorption by >50%; Ca2+ addition at 0.2 mM and stated Ca:TC ratio 2:1 increased sorption above pH 5 (PDF p.5).
- Table 2 gives sorption/desorption Freundlich coefficients and hysteresis x=7.61 at 0.01 M and x=5.38 at 0.1 M (PDF p.7). Kf units are not printed in the table.
- Use for speciation/NOM mechanisms, not as a qmax value for solid adsorbents.

### P02 — Masrura et al. (2022)

- The urine matrix was fresh volunteer urine diluted 50:50 with DI water and spiked with AZ, CPX, SMX, TMP and TC; NH4+ and phosphate were co-solutes (PDF pp.3–4).
- OW900 model-reported Langmuir Qm (µg/g): AZ 290.06, TMP 299.19, TC 322.75, SMX 281.47, CPX 389.00. Langmuir r² is weaker for AZ (0.70) and SMX (0.67); paper says antibiotic data were generally better fitted by Freundlich (PDF p.5, Table 2).
- PMS400 model-reported Langmuir Qm (µg/g): TMP 280.97, TC 199.53, SMX 246.70, CPX 283.69; several Langmuir r² values are 0.51–0.63. AZ is absent from Table 2 because authors say 100% removal occurred at very low dose.
- Antibiotic uptake shows a fast 0–6 h phase then slower 6–24 h (PDF p.7). OW900 desorption into DI water was 92.6% for TMP and 3.3% for SMX; AZ desorption was 10% despite 100% reported adsorption (PDF pp.8–9).
- Processing discrepancy: Table 1 says 900 °C for OW900, but Methods describe 950 °C for 2 h under N2 (PDF p.4). Do not resolve by guess.

### P03 — Alawa et al. (2025)

- WS was pyrolyzed at 700 °C for 2 h under N2 to produce CWS; AWS involved H3PO4 dry impregnation (CWS:H3PO4=1:3) at 105 °C for 48 h followed by recalcination at 700 °C for 2 h (PDF p.3).
- Table 1 reports SBET 28/273/219 m²/g for WS/CWS/AWS and pore volume 0.074/0.22/0.27 cm³/g (PDF p.6). Activation decreased measured SBET from 273 to 219 m²/g while raising pore volume and pore diameter.
- At a common dose of 1 g/L and 30 min, pH 7 results are 42.16% CWS and 79.78% AWS (PDF p.8); optimized-dose section reports 85.22% CWS at 1.6 g/L and 98.2% AWS at 1.0 g/L (PDF p.6); contact-time section reports 89.85% CWS at about 120 min and 98.2% AWS around 50 min (PDF p.8). Keep conditions attached.
- AWS 14.51 mg/g and CWS 8.56 mg/g imply roughly 69.5% relative capacity increase, inconsistent with the separate 36.31–43.59% claim. Preserve both as an unresolved discrepancy (PDF p.8, §4.1.4).
- Kinetic-model statement is ambiguous (PSO r²=0.999 yet NLIPDM also said to fit better); supporting Table S2 is missing. Table S4 thermodynamic parameters also not uploaded. No multi-cycle regeneration data in the main text; authors say regeneration needs further study.

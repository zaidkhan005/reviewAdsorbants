## Candidate-specific source pins

### CLM-53 — Yu et al. (2017), magnetic GO sponge; DOI 10.1016/j.matchemphys.2017.05.042

- **Key:** `yuAdsorptionBehaviorsTetracycline2017`.
- **Source tier:** official ScienceDirect abstract/preview, Crossref metadata and ORCID publication listing. Full Methods/tables remain access-gated.
- **Disposition:** author-described capacity 473 mg/g remains metric-unclassified; no complete operating conditions are visible. Do not call it observed q_e or fitted q_max, and do not rank it. A third-party index returned a “withdrawn” label, but the record behind it was inaccessible and no official publisher/Crossref retraction update was found. This is unverified, not evidence of retraction.
- **Evidence row:** EV-SEM-019.

### CLM-54 — Li et al. (2018), DTPA-functionalized magnetic GO; DOI 10.1016/j.jcis.2018.03.003

- **Key:** `liPerformanceMagneticGraphene2018`.
- **Source tier:** university-hosted primary 10-page PDF; Methods §§2.2/2.4 and Results §§3.2–3.9, printed pp. 151–158. SI Tables S1–S3 unavailable and rendered figure verification failed.
- **Disposition:** PSO q_e fits 294.12 mg/g (TC) and 111.73 mg/g (CIP) are model-derived, not measured q_e or q_max. Feed ceiling for the single-solute kinetic run is 555.6 mg/g. The authors report Freundlich as the better isotherm fit. TC/CIP binary effects are asymmetric. Four-cycle 0.01 M NaOH performance (>78% removal) is not cycle-resolved TC capacity retention. No real wastewater or normalized cost data.
- **Evidence row:** EV-SEM-008.

### CLM-55 — Bao et al. (2018), MnFe₂O₄-decorated rGO; DOI 10.1186/s11671-018-2814-9

- **Key:** `baoAdsorptionTetracyclineReduced2018`.
- **Source tier:** official PMC/Springer full-text HTML and original 8-page PDF; Methods, Fig. 4, Table 1, Fig. 5/Table 2 and Fig. 6.
- **Disposition:** reported ~41 mg/g TC uptake at C₀=10 mg/L, 30 mL and 5 mg adsorbent is below the 60 mg/g feed ceiling; pH was not stated for that kinetic test. The separate pH series reports 53.6 mg/g at pH 3.3. Langmuir q_m=870–1326 mg/g is fitted; Freundlich has higher R². 0.1 M HCl reuse values retain a Methods/Figure cycle-count discrepancy.
- **Evidence row:** EV-SEM-003.

### CLM-56 — Miao et al. (2019), magnetic GO for tetracyclines; DOI 10.1016/j.apsusc.2019.01.036

- **Key:** `miaoAdsorptionPerformanceTetracyclines2019`.
- **Source tier:** official ScienceDirect abstract/Highlights and selected snippets; full Methods/tables gated.
- **Disposition:** use qualitative model and target-specific pH results only. PSO and Freundlich are described as better fits; TC-HCl and OTC pH optima near 3.3, while CTC differs. No primary numerical capacity or complete test conditions are accessible. The 141.44 mg/g number occurs in a secondary review, not the accessible primary preview.
- **Evidence row:** EV-SEM-034.

### CLM-57 — Saya et al. (2023), magnetic guar-gum/GO/Fe₃O₄ for CIP; DOI 10.1016/j.jece.2023.110766

- **Key:** `sayaAdsorptiveRemovalCiprofloxacin2023`.
- **Source tier:** official ScienceDirect and author-posted SSRN abstracts; detailed text comes from a third-party Scribd mirror because the SSRN PDF was unavailable.
- **Disposition:** 555.56 mg/g is a mirror-derived Langmuir-fitted q_max, not observed q_e. Table/prose R² values differ (.9915/.9989). Baseline dose (0.5 g/L) conflicts tenfold with a stated dose-response optimum (5 g/L); exact isotherm dose is not pinned. Six-cycle ~95% adsorption efficiency is not q-capacity retention. Hold quantitative ranking.
- **Evidence row:** EV-SEM-010.

### CLM-58 — Da Silva Bruckmann et al. (2022), magnetic GO·Fe₃O₄ for TC; DOI 10.1016/j.molliq.2022.118837

- **Key:** `dasilvabruckmannDFTTheoreticalExperimental2022`.
- **Source tier:** official ScienceDirect abstract/Highlights and DFT/conclusion snippets; no full text in ResearchGate or current Library.
- **Disposition:** 531.93 mg/g remains an author-reported maximum with unresolved metric class. The visible conditions include 1:1 GO·Fe₃O₄, 50 mg adsorbent, C₀=1000 mg/L, pH 7 and 293 K, but solution volume/residual concentration/contact time are absent. At least 26.6 mL feed is required at 100% removal for the implied adsorbed mass. DFT binding energy/spin/HOMO-LUMO values remain theoretical, separate from empirical uptake. Do not label the maximum Langmuir q_max or rank.
- **Evidence row:** EV-SEM-030.

### CLM-59 — Lily et al. (2026), EDTA-functionalized magnetic GO for TC; DOI 10.1016/j.dwt.2026.101889

- **Key:** `lilyEDTAfunctionalizedMagneticGraphene2026`.
- **Source tier:** primary full-text indexed copy with precise page/table/figure pins: Methods §2, pp. 2–4; Fig. 3 p. 7; Table 2/Fig. 4 p. 8; Table 3 p. 9; Fig. 6 p. 10. Raw data are unavailable by author statement.
- **Disposition:** 93.23% removal at C₀=.2 mg/L, 20 mL, 0.1 g/L dose, pH 5, 5 min and 25 °C implies derived q_e≈1.865 mg/g under a 2 mg/g feed ceiling. PFO/PSO fitted q_e values are inconsistent with that check; the 25 °C Langmuir fit is weak. Eight-cycle efficiency (~94→90.50%) must not be labelled capacity retention. No native wastewater or independently reproduced cost result.
- **Evidence row:** EV-SEM-028.

### CLM-60 — Yang et al. (2017), thiourea-dioxide-reduced magnetic GO for TC; DOI 10.1016/j.jcis.2017.01.075

- **Key:** `yangDecontaminationTetracyclineThioureadioxidereduced2017`.
- **Source tier:** official ScienceDirect abstract/Materials/Characterization/Conclusion snippets and PubMed record; full primary text unavailable.
- **Disposition:** 1233 mg/g at 313 K is a Langmuir-fitted q_m, not observed q_e. Primary isotherm range, dose, volume and fit statistics are missing. A secondary review's 10 mg/L, 70 g/L vector must not be attached to the primary fitted q_m; a second review has an incorrect reference mapping in one table. Use only qualitative pH/NaCl/humic-acid context.
- **Evidence row:** EV-SEM-038.

### CLM-61 — Wang et al. (2016), magnetic chitosan-grafted GO for CIP; DOI 10.1016/j.molliq.2016.07.037

- **Key:** `wangRemovalCiprofloxacinAqueous2016`.
- **Source tier:** official ScienceDirect abstract/Highlights and selected snippets; ResearchGate states no full text; detailed Methods/tables unavailable.
- **Disposition:** 282.9 mg/g is Langmuir-fitted q_m, not observed q_e. The preview reports PSO kinetics and about 72% initial capacity retained after four uses, but complete conditions, fit statistics, regenerant and per-cycle data are unavailable. Do not condition-rank or infer low-cost process performance.
- **Evidence row:** EV-SEM-033.

## Rules retained

1. Adsorbent characterization is not automatically direct evidence of an adsorbed-state bond.
2. Before/after spectra and pH/speciation response support hypotheses; they rarely quantify force contributions.
3. DFT/MD, D–R energy, and kinetic/isotherm fits are model outputs, not direct measurements of bond energy or proof of chemisorption.
4. Magnetic separation is an operational recovery function, not a molecular adsorption mechanism.
5. Source-access tier limits the permitted strength of mechanistic claims.

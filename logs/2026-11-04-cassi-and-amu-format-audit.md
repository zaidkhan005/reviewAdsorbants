# CASSI journal-abbreviation audit and AMU format check — 4 November 2026

## Scope and method

The active bibliography contains 60 records covering 40 distinct full journal titles. Each title/abbreviation pair was checked against the publicly indexed CAS Source Index (CASSI) Search Tool records, using title and/or ISSN-specific searches where the result was available. CASSI describes its service as the place to confirm publication titles and abbreviated titles and documents both title/abbreviation and ISSN searching. Its indexed database was reported as updated 25 October 2026 at the time of this check.

**Outcome:** 30 of the 40 abbreviated titles have an exact corresponding CASSI record in the accessible search results. Ten titles could not be confirmed from the accessible CASSI result set. They remain provisional; absence of a returned search result is not treated as proof that the journal is absent from the full CASSI database.

## Official AMU undergraduate-format and citation-style check

The AMU Department of Chemistry's official undergraduate page identifies the current programme as B.Sc. (NEP) and B.Sc. Chemistry and lists syllabus/curriculum links. It does not publish a manuscript template, report/dissertation layout, reference style, or citation-style instruction on the accessible page.

The AMU Research and Development Cell page lists procedures for research-project proposal submission and execution. Those are institutional research-administration procedures, not an undergraduate semester review-manuscript style guide. The publicly available Ph.D. thesis regulations concern doctoral theses and should not be applied to a B.Sc. semester paper.

**Conclusion:** no authoritative public AMU instruction establishing ACS, APA, Vancouver, or another citation style for this specific B.Sc. Chemistry semester review was located. ACS remains a workable current manuscript style, not a confirmed AMU requirement. The Department of Chemistry/supervisor must confirm the required format before final institutional submission.

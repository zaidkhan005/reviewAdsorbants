## Individual source pins and permitted use

### CLM-62 — Padmaja and Sri Chandana (2024), DOI 10.1088/1742-6596/2779/1/012098

- **Key:** `padmajaAdsorptiveRemovalCiprofloxacin2024`.
- **Source:** official IOP proceedings record and open-access 13-page PDF: https://doi.org/10.1088/1742-6596/2779/1/012098
- **Pins:** §§3.1–3.3, §4.2/Fig. 4, Table 1, §4.4/Table 2, §4.5/Fig. 7 and Conclusions.
- **Audit:** At C0=10 mg/L, 1 g/L, neutral pH and 20 min, Fig. 4 reports 34% removal for GO and 31.5% for GO–TiO2. Table 1 source-labelled qe, fitted Langmuir Q0 and kinetic fits remain held because the GO–TiO2 2 g/L Table 1 qe=4.08 mg/g conflicts with Fig. 4; uptake continues after 20 min despite the text's equilibrium claim; and kinetic fit prose conflicts with Table 1. 
- **Disposition:** use only the 20-minute removal endpoint, with its conditions and conference-proceedings designation. No real-water, regeneration or quantified cost data.

### CLM-63 — Fei et al. (2016), DOI 10.1016/j.jcis.2016.08.068

- **Key:** `feiAdsorptiveRemovalCiprofloxacin2024`.
- **Source:** official ScienceDirect/PubMed abstract; detailed tables transcribed from a third-party-hosted author-accepted manuscript, not publisher-hosted full text.
- **Pins:** accepted-manuscript §§2.2–2.4; Table 2 kinetics; Table 3 isotherms; §3.4 pH and ionic strength.
- **Audit:** Table 2 experimental kinetic qe=25.86 mg/g (GO-SA hydrogel) and22.59 mg/g (aerogel); PSO fitted qe=25.38/22.68 mg/g; Table 3 Langmuir fitted qmax=86.12/55.55 mg/g. These are different endpoints. Kinetic test: C0=50mg/L,50mL,15mg GO-SA,298K,180rpm. Isotherm series:10–150mg/L,15mg dry GO-SA/50mL,298K,180rpm,128h. Exact GO:SA ratio is not explicitly disclosed.
- **Disposition:** partial evidence, with detailed tables explicitly labelled accepted-manuscript/third-party-hosted; do not describe qmax as observed uptake. No regeneration cycles, real water or cost evidence.

### CLM-64 — Choi and Choi (2022), DOI 10.3390/toxics10020077

- **Key:** `choiPolyacrylamideFunctionalizedGraphene2022`.
- **Source:** official MDPI/PMC full text: https://www.mdpi.com/2305-6304/10/2/77
- **Pins:** §§2.1–2.4, Figs. 1–6, Tables 1–3 and Conclusion.
- **Audit:** Table 1 Langmuir q_m=6.846 mg/g is fitted; no directly tabulated observed qe/removal is provided. Methods describe 55mL liquid in a 50mL conical tube and the qe equation uses V in mL with concentration in ppm (mg/L), without an explicit 1/1000 conversion. Do not reverse-engineer observed qe. TGA thermal stability is not a regeneration test.
- **Disposition:** use fitted q_m only with the vessel/equation caveats; no regeneration, real-water or quantified cost evidence.

### CLM-65 — Wu et al. (2013), DOI 10.1016/j.cej.2013.06.072

- **Key:** `wuAdsorptionCiprofloxacinBiocomposite2013`.
- **Source:** official ScienceDirect publisher abstract, Highlights, Introduction, Chemicals/Reagents, characterization snippets and Conclusions: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1385894713008450
- **Audit:** publisher reports pH optimum5.9 and equilibrium time24h, plus a separate 78.9% removal at2g/L and6wt.% GO. It separately reports capacity18.45→39.06mg/g as GO loading rises0→6wt.% at equilibrium CIP concentration60mg/L. The preview does not demonstrate that the removal and capacity are the same experiment, nor provide the complete matched C0, volume and temperature. 39.06mg/g is an author-reported equilibrium capacity, not fitted qmax.
- **Disposition:** provisional/partial publisher-preview evidence. Keep the two endpoints separate; no regeneration, real-water or cost data are available in the preview.

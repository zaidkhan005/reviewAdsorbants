## Study identity
Miao, J., Wang, F., Chen, Y., Zhu, Y., Zhou, Y. and Zhang, S. (2019), “The adsorption performance of tetracyclines on magnetic graphene oxide: A novel antibiotics absorbent,” *Applied Surface Science* 475, 549–558. DOI: 10.1016/j.apsusc.2019.01.036.

Official ScienceDirect publisher page: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0169433219300455
Secondary review: “A review on tetracycline removal from aqueous systems by advanced treatment techniques,” *RSC Advances* (2020), DOI: 10.1039/D0RA04264A, §4.2.3, reference 77: https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlehtml/2020/ra/d0ra04264a

The ScienceDirect preview and the article's highlights, abstract, Introduction, Materials, Synthesis of MGO, Characterization and Conclusion snippets were checked on 31 October 2026. Full tables and detailed Methods remain behind institutional access/purchase. Existing Library search did not find a matching full-text PDF; no new PDF batch is assumed or requested.

## Targets must remain separate
The paper studies three tetracycline antibiotics as distinct adsorbate targets:
- Tetracycline hydrochloride (TC-HCl; paper labels it TC)
- Oxytetracycline (OTC)
- Chlortetracycline (CTC)

The publisher abstract states:
- TC-HCl and OTC show their best adsorption around pH 3.3.
- CTC performs better under alkaline conditions.
- Adsorption is described as spontaneous and endothermic.
- Pseudo-second-order kinetics and Freundlich isotherm are reported to correlate better with the experimental data.
- The authors describe MGO as an easy-recycling adsorbent.

Do not pool the three drug capacities or assume that TC-HCl's pH optimum applies to CTC.

## What the primary preview does and does not report
Accessible primary publisher text does not expose:
- a numerical TC-HCl removal percentage or qe at a named condition;
- a numerical primary qmax or isotherm fit table;
- the exact C0 range, solution volume, MGO mass/dose, temperature, contact time or equilibrium definition;
- PSO/PFO/Freundlich model parameters or goodness-of-fit statistics;
- full Fe3O4 preparation/loading details or Fe3O4:GO ratio;
- a desorption/regeneration procedure, number of cycles or retained capacity;
- actual wastewater/real-water performance, metal leaching, product yield, energy use or monetized cost.

The abstract/hilights say Pseudo-second-order and Freundlich fit better, but coefficients and numerical fits are not available in the preview. “Easy-recycling” is a qualitative statement, not cycle-resolved reuse evidence.

## Preparation snippet
The accessible Methods snippet states:
- Expandable graphite oxide (EGO) is produced from graphite powder via a modified Hummers method, with details referenced to earlier work.
- 100 mg EGO is dispersed in 30 mL N-methyl pyrrolidone (NMP) using room-temperature ultrasonication.
- The remaining Fe3O4 composite preparation is not visible in the accessible snippet.
- Characterization methods include SEM, BET, FTIR and XPS.

NMP is a processing solvent; recovery, solvent use per gram, product yield, energy, waste treatment and cost are not reported in the preview. Do not infer the exact magnetic loading recipe from another MGO paper.

## The 141.44 mg/g value — secondary-source only
A 2020 RSC Advances review, §4.2.3, states that MGO has a tetracycline adsorption capacity of 141.44 mg/g and cites Miao et al. (2019) as its reference 77. The official primary ScienceDirect page preview was separately checked, including a targeted extraction prompt; 141.44 mg/g does **not** appear anywhere in the accessible primary abstract, highlights, Introduction, synthesis/characterization or conclusion snippets.

Therefore:
- preserve 141.44 mg/g as a secondary-review-attributed value only;
- do not claim that it has been verified from the primary article;
- do not assign it to measured qe, Langmuir qmax or another model parameter;
- do not infer its C0, dose, volume, pH, temperature or contact time;
- do not enter it in the condition-labelled comparison or numeric capacity ranking.

The primary paper may contain the value in its gated numerical tables, but the accessible evidence does not permit that confirmation. It remains pending full-text verification.

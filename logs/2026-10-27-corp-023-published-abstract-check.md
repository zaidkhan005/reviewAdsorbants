- The abstract states external diffusion governs alginate adsorption and internal diffusion governs chitosan adsorption.

The headline pH/capacity values match the preprint-derived evidence in `EV-B20-005`. A new source-linked audit entry, `EV-AUD-006`, was added to `data/corpus-evidence.csv`. ## Still unverified

The accessible published material did **not** establish whether every preprint method, fitted-isotherm parameter, kinetic diffusion asymptote, thermodynamic result, or supplementary table is unchanged. In particular:

- The preprint reports alginate kinetic uptake of 71.3 mg/g, while Table 2 reports fitted Weber–Morris (q_infty = 136.75 pm 8.2) mg/g.
- The preprint lists Langmuir (q_{max}) values while saying Freundlich generally fit better.
- Detailed method controls relating to the commercial capsule's active ingredient/excipients remain a caveat until the published text is reviewed.

Do not silently replace the preprint's detailed extraction with the abstract. Use the headline capacities only with the published abstract citation; keep detailed model and method values provisional until the full published article and supplement can be inspected.

## Individual source pins

### CLM-81 — Dipannita et al. (2025), DOI 10.1039/D5RA06621B

- **Key:** `dipannitaCarboxymethylcelluloseFunctionalizedMagnetic2025`; evidence EV-SEM-007.
- **Pins:** official RSC full text, Materials §§2.3–2.6 (printed pp.35220–35222), Results §§3.2.1–3.2.4, Fig.4, Tables 2–3, Table 4/Fig.7/§3.2.8.
- **Preparation/recovery:** CMC-functionalized magnetic GO/Fe3O4; heat treatment and 18 h vacuum drying; external magnetic separation demonstrated.
- **Reuse/matrix:** ten methanol cycles reduce removal from ~96% to ~92%; this is removal efficiency, not a q_e retention series. Synthetic TC water, no native real-water test. Methanol recovery/disposal, yields, energy and full cost are not quantified.

### CLM-82 — Hossain et al. (2026), DOI 10.1039/D6RA00494F

- **Key:** `hossainRemovalCiprofloxacinAqueous2026`; evidence EV-SEM-026.
- **Pins:** official RSC/PMC full text, preparation §§2.2–2.4 and practicality/economic sections §§3.10–3.13.
- **Preparation/recovery:** GO/PVA/calcium-alginate beads; batch filtration/centrifugation, no demonstrated magnetic or continuous-flow recovery.
- **Reuse/matrix:** authors describe 4–5 cycles with <25% capacity loss, but a fully matched cycle-by-cycle mass balance was not pinned. Synthetic aqueous CIP; no native wastewater evidence. The cost scenario's 0.1 g/L dose differs 100-fold from a key 10 g/L experiment; the monetary implication is separately audited in CLM-73.

### CLM-83 — Shaha et al. (2026), DOI 10.1016/j.rineng.2026.110064

- **Key:** `shahaKineticIsothermThermodynamic2026`; evidence EV-SEM-036.
- **Pins:** official open-access ScienceDirect full article and supplement; Methods §2, Tables 1–5, Figs. 2–12, mixture and regeneration sections.
- **Preparation/recovery:** strong-acid/oxidant Hummers oxidation, sulfonation, ethanol/diethyl-ether washes; DMF + 1 M HCl regeneration.
- **Reuse/matrix:** >97% release in 10 min and >92% capacity retention over five cycles, but all at the equimolar mixed-fluoroquinolone level, not CIP-specific. Article explicitly says real wastewater was not tested. Solvent recovery, treatment and total synthesis costs are unavailable.

### CLM-84 — Li et al. (2025), DOI 10.1016/j.psep.2025.107659

- **Key:** `liThreedimensionalGrapheneOxidebased2025`; evidence EV-SEM-013.
- **Pins:** existing Library primary PDF, printed pp.4–12; §2.6 references supporting information.
- **Preparation/recovery:** multi-step GO oxidation, phosphorus doping, TETA grafting, EDC/NHS coupling, beta-cyclodextrin acrylation in DMF/triethylamine/acryloyl chloride and hydrogel polymerization at 75 °C. The hydrogel format immobilizes the adsorbent; large-scale recovery/loss cost is not measured.
- **Reuse/matrix:** seven methanol adsorption–desorption cycles are reported, with about 90.53% of initial removal retained after cycle seven; preserve the source's wording. Figure/application matrix labels say simulated real wastewater while prose says real-water samples; retain conflict. No yield, energy or normalized cost inventory.

### CLM-85 — Hossain et al. (2024), DOI 10.1039/D3RA07597D

- **Key:** `hossainUltrafastSimultaneousRemoval2024`; evidence EV-SEM-027.
- **Pins:** official RSC/PMC full text, §§2.1–2.4, §3.2 and reuse §3.2.7.
- **Preparation/recovery:** spent dry-cell graphite cleaned with aqua regia, improved-Hummers oxidation, Co/Fe salts, pH adjustment, heating and ethanol/water washing/drying; magnetic separation demonstrated.
- **Reuse/matrix:** methanol regeneration and 50 °C drying over up to eight rounds; overall removal ~95%→90%, not TC-specific capacity retention. Main TC run is synthetic water (0.2 mg/L, pH 7, 30 s); no real-water/coexisting-matrix test, yield, energy or normalized cost evidence.

### CLM-86 — Zhang and Wu (2022), DOI 10.1002/jeq2.20415

- **Key:** `zhangAdsorptionCharacteristicsNrGO2022`; CORP-031 and EV-B10-004–009 reused without duplicate extraction.
- **Pins:** user-supplied Wiley PDF, Methods §2.3, characterization, Figs. 2–3, Tables 1–2 and matrix/regeneration sections.
- **Preparation/recovery:** strong-acid/oxidant GO oxidation, 100 W ultrasonication, ammonia-assisted hydrothermal nitrogen doping at 180 °C for 24 h, repeated washing/drying. Regeneration uses 0.1 M HCl elution and 0.1 M NaOH treatment, followed by DI washing.
- **Reuse/matrix:** up to eight cycles with an overall capacity decline stated as no more than 25% across antibiotics; no TC-specific per-cycle series. Ion/HA trials are simulated matrices, not native real water; 10 mg/L HA reduces uptake up to 30%. The TC 80 mL feed + 20 mL dispersion basis and fitted-q_m anomalies remain unresolved. No yield, reagent recovery, energy or cost denominator is reported.

## Interpretation safeguards

- Keep removal efficiency, capacity retention and desorption efficiency separate.
- Do not attribute mixed-antibiotic cycle-retention results to individual CIP or TC unless the source reports target-specific values.
- Simulated ion/HA or synthetic wastewater tests are not native real-water validation.
- Magnetic separation, beads and monolithic/hydrogel formats may improve handling but do not establish lower total process cost without yields, energy, reagents, washing, regeneration and adsorbent-loss balances.

## Study design and material identity
The study evaluates graphene oxide/[Ni(dimethylglyoxime)(DMG)]n coordination composites for adsorption of ciprofloxacin from aqueous solution. The publisher lists three labelled formulations:
- Ni–DMG@GO-A
- Ni–DMG@GO-B
- Ni–DMG@GO-C

The publisher conclusion gives their BET surface areas:
- A: 0.43 m²/g
- B: 0.73 m²/g
- C: 2.40 m²/g

Do not infer the Langmuir qmax-to-formulation mapping from the BET surface-area sequence. The preview does not say which formulation corresponds to117, which to159mg/g, or whether the two endpoints refer to particular formulations or a range across all three.

## Adsorption performance and model hierarchy
The official abstract/highlights report:
- Langmuir comparative adsorption-capacity range:117–159mg CIP/g adsorbent.
- High removal over pH2–7, without a numerical removal percentage.
- Pseudo-second-order (PSO) as the best-fitting kinetic model.
- Freundlich and Temkin as the best-fitting equilibrium isotherm models.
- Langmuir is additionally reported for comparative maximum-capacity evaluation because its R² values are reasonably high.

Important distinctions:
1. The117–159mg/g range is Langmuir-derived comparative qmax, not an observed qe.
2. It is not mapped to formulations A/B/C in the publisher preview.
3. Freundlich/Temkin are the reported better equilibrium descriptions, so the Langmuir numbers should not be presented as proof of a Langmuir-preferred adsorption mechanism or a directly measured maximum uptake.
4. PSO is the best kinetic fit, but a PSO fit by itself does not prove chemisorption.
5. Exact model coefficients, R² values, error metrics and fit residuals are not exposed in the accessible text.

The conclusion adds PSO rate constants in the range0.0005–0.0009g/(mg·min). It does not map the constants to each A/B/C formulation in the available preview.

## pH and mechanism
The abstract/highlights report high CIP removal over pH2–7, but do not provide a numerical pH-response table, a single exact pH optimum, or a removal percentage linked to a complete batch condition. The proposed mechanisms are electrostatic and π–π interactions. The preview does not provide the necessary pHpzc, zeta-potential values, CIP concentration/speciation table, surface-group contribution analysis or formulation-resolved mechanistic evidence to quantitatively attribute the adsorption pathways.

The pH statement must not be rewritten as a confirmed 2–7 optimum under one complete condition set; it is a broad range over which the authors report high removal.

## Semester-paper decision
Retain SEM-030 as qualitative/source-level evidence for a GO–Ni(DMG) coordination composite, its three formulations and BET surface areas, and the authors' reported model hierarchy.

For quantitative synthesis:
1. Preserve117–159mg/g strictly as an author-reported, Langmuir-derived comparative qmax range.
2. Do not call it observed qe or map values to A/B/C.
3. State that Freundlich and Temkin fit the equilibrium data better; Langmuir is used as a comparative maximum-capacity metric.
4. Preserve PSO rate constant range0.0005–0.0009g/(mg·min) without inventing formulation mappings.
5. Report pH2–7 as the broad high-removal range, not a complete optimum condition.
6. Do not add a condition-labelled numeric comparison row until the full Methods and result tables are available.
7. Do not infer reusability, real-water applicability, metal-leaching safety or low-cost production.

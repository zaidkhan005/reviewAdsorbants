# Citation and claim-audit rules

Before any manuscript claim is accepted:

1. **Locate the original source.** A review can guide discovery but should not substitute for the original paper when reporting experimental values.
2. **Check the exact material.** Do not merge raw, activated, chemically modified and composite adsorbents.
3. **Check the exact metric.** Keep removal percentage, observed equilibrium uptake, fitted qmax and partition/kinetic parameters separate.
4. **Check units.** Preserve the paper's units, record conversions explicitly, and show calculations in the evidence note when converting.
5. **Check conditions.** Record pH, concentration, dose, contact time, temperature, background electrolyte, water matrix and competing solutes.
6. **Check mechanism strength.** Label mechanisms as experimentally supported, model-inferred, author-proposed or uncertain.
7. **Check process type.** Adsorption, catalytic oxidation, photodegradation, biodegradation and membrane rejection are different processes.
8. **Check Q1 status independently.** Record ranking source, year, category, quartile and supporting URL.
9. **Check limitations.** Record missing real-water tests, regeneration cycles, column runs, energy/cost analysis and uncertainty.
10. **Insert citation with Zotero.** Never rely on hand-typed bibliographic citations as the final reference-management workflow.

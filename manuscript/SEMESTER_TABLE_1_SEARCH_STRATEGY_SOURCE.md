# Table 1. Search strategy and sources

The searches below were used to identify primary studies of graphene-based adsorbents for ciprofloxacin (CIP) and tetracycline (TC) removal from aqueous media. The queries combine adsorbent identity, target antibiotic and adsorption or treatment concepts.

| Search ID | Search focus | Search string |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Broad GO/rGO and both target antibiotics | `("graphene oxide" OR "reduced graphene oxide") AND ciprofloxacin AND tetracycline AND adsorption AND ("magnetic graphene composite")` |
| 2 | GO and CIP in aqueous solution | `graphene oxide adsorption ciprofloxacin aqueous solution primary study DOI` |
| 3 | Magnetic GO and TC | `magnetic graphene oxide tetracycline adsorption primary study DOI` |
| 4 | rGO and both antibiotics | `reduced graphene oxide tetracycline ciprofloxacin adsorption 2024 study` |
| 5 | Graphene–polymer composites and CIP | `graphene polymer composite ciprofloxacin adsorption aqueous primary research` |
| 6 | Sulfonated GO and CIP | `sulfonated graphene oxide ciprofloxacin adsorption 2025` |
| 7 | GO and CIP in real water/wastewater | `graphene oxide ciprofloxacin adsorption real water wastewater study` |
| 8 | Magnetic graphene, TC and process outcomes | `tetracycline magnetic graphene oxide composite adsorption regeneration column study` |
| 9 | PubMed-focused GO/CIP search | `site:PubMed.gov graphene oxide ciprofloxacin adsorption` |
| 10 | PubMed-focused GO/TC search | `site:PubMed.gov tetracycline graphene oxide adsorption` |
| 11 | Both targets, 2017–2026 | `graphene-based tetracycline ciprofloxacin adsorption primary research 2017–2026` |
| 12 | 2026 GO/CIP studies | `2026 graphene oxide adsorption ciprofloxacin water adsorbent magnetic composite` |
| 13 | 2026 TC and magnetic/MOF composites | `2026 tetracycline adsorption graphene oxide composite magnetic MOF water adsorption` |
| 14 | 2025 GO/rGO studies for both targets | `2025 graphene oxide reduced graphene oxide ciprofloxacin tetracycline adsorption aqueous primary study` |

Additional targeted searches examined magnetic GO/Co-MOF composites for TC, ZrO₂–Fe₃O₄/GO composites for CIP, and GO/[Ni(DMG)]ₙ composites for CIP. Search wording was varied for these targeted follow-up searches rather than treated as one fixed query.

**Sources searched and coverage boundaries**

| Source channel | Use in study identification | Coverage note |
|---|---|---|
| Public web searches | Broad discovery and targeted follow-up searches | Focused rather than exhaustive |
| PubMed and PubMed Central (PMC) | Biomedical indexing and accessible full-text records | Used where relevant records were available |
| Publisher platforms | RSC Publishing, Frontiers, ScienceDirect and other publisher websites | Article pages and accessible full text were checked |
| Institutional and author repositories | Retrieval of hosted manuscripts and author copies | Used where available; source tier was recorded |
| Scopus and Web of Science | Not searched | Outside the search coverage |

No fixed publication-date or language limits were applied. The search was intended to identify relevant studies for a focused narrative review, not to capture every publication on antibiotic adsorption.

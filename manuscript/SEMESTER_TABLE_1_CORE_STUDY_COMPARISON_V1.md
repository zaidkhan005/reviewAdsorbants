## Table notes and rules

1. **Observed qe** means an experimental uptake endpoint reported by the source. A value calculated from stated feed concentration, removal percentage, volume and adsorbent mass is labelled *derived*; it is not silently presented as a source-reported observed qe.
2. **Fitted qmax/qm** and **kinetic-model-fitted qe** are separate model outputs. Neither is treated as measured uptake or proof of a molecular mechanism.
3. Removal percentages are attached to their own conditions and are not combined with uptake values from separate experiments.
4. Single-solute, multicomponent, trace-level, simulated-matrix, real-water and PRB/column results are separate contexts. GAC is only a benchmark.
5. Several studies contain source-internal inconsistencies or incomplete access. Those caveats remain visible and prevent a universal capacity ranking.

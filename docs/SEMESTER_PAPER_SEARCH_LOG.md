# Targeted literature search log — semester paper

**Search date:** 28 October 2026 (Asia/Kolkata)  
**Scope searched:** Selected graphene-based adsorbents and graphene-bearing composites for ciprofloxacin (CIP) and tetracycline (TC) adsorption in aqueous media.  
**Purpose:** Discovery and screening, not an exhaustive systematic-review search. Search-engine results and publisher/biomedical records were used to identify primary-study leads; no database-completeness claim is made from this pass.

## 1. Search strings used

The focused public-web search pass used combinations equivalent to:

1. `("graphene oxide" OR "reduced graphene oxide") AND ciprofloxacin AND tetracycline AND adsorption AND ("magnetic graphene composite")`
2. `graphene oxide adsorption ciprofloxacin aqueous solution primary study DOI`
3. `magnetic graphene oxide tetracycline adsorption primary study DOI`
4. `reduced graphene oxide tetracycline ciprofloxacin adsorption 2024 study`
5. `graphene polymer composite ciprofloxacin adsorption aqueous primary research`
6. `sulfonated graphene oxide ciprofloxacin adsorption 2025`
7. `graphene oxide ciprofloxacin adsorption real water wastewater study`
8. `tetracycline magnetic graphene oxide composite adsorption regeneration column study`
9. `site:PubMed.gov graphene oxide ciprofloxacin adsorption`
10. `site:PubMed.gov tetracycline graphene oxide adsorption`
11. `graphene-based tetracycline ciprofloxacin adsorption primary research 2017–2026`

Searches located studies in PubMed/PMC, RSC Publishing, Frontiers, and ScienceDirect. The precise source URL, DOI when verified, target drug(s), preliminary fit, and next action are recorded in `data/semester-paper-candidate-register.csv`.

## 2. What the first pass establishes

- The core pairing has direct-study precedent: one magnetic, functionalized graphene-oxide composite has been studied for both CIP and TC in single-solute and binary systems (Li et al., 2018; DOI 10.1016/j.jcis.2018.03.003).
- Family A is represented by graphene oxide and reduced graphene oxide studies that investigate CIP and/or TC.
- Family B is represented by magnetic GO/rGO, graphene–alginate/cellulose/polymer composites, and a GO–layered-double-hydroxide composite.
- New candidate leads from 2025–2026 exist, including functionalized magnetic graphene-oxide materials for TC; these need the same full-text scrutiny as older work.
- The candidate register separates **provisional in-scope leads**, **needs clarification**, **context-only reviews**, and **discovery leads requiring exact metadata**. Known duplicates are merged; records with unresolved metadata may still represent duplicates and must be verified. None should be treated as finally included solely on title/abstract evidence.

## 3. Screening warnings

1. Candidate rows represent distinct study records after merging known DOI/title duplicates. The planned-pool sulfonated-GO lead POOL-024 is merged into the verified sulfonated-GO record, not counted as another study. DOI and title matching must still be completed for records whose metadata remain unresolved.
2. Tetracycline-family drugs must not be silently pooled. A paper on oxytetracycline, chlortetracycline, or doxycycline is not automatically a TC study.
3. Papers using ultrasound, oxidants, photocatalysis, Fenton-like processes, or other coupled pathways need an adsorption-specific endpoint before inclusion.
4. A theoretical or DFT-only paper can support a mechanism discussion but should not substitute for measured aqueous adsorption-performance data in the main performance table.
5. Distinguish measured uptake from fitted `qmax`, percent removal, and model-derived parameters; always record the relevant conditions and source location.
6. No journal quartile has been asserted by this search log. The separate journal-ranking audit must be joined after confirming journal title, year and category/provider.
7. “Low-cost” remains an analytical question. A waste precursor is not sufficient evidence of low total preparation cost; likewise magnetic separation and regeneration can add costs or reagent burdens.

## 8. Rerun cautions and exclusions surfaced

- A 2026 corn-cob-derived GO short communication concerns dispersive solid-phase extraction for analytical determination of CIP. It may be useful contextual literature but should not automatically count as water-treatment removal evidence because its purpose and operating context are analytical preconcentration.
- A 2025 SnO₂/rGO tetracycline paper is framed primarily around photodegradation under visible light; exclude it from core adsorption evidence unless the paper supplies an adsorption-only endpoint/control that can be separated.
- The newly surfaced 2026 papers are useful for a current literature map, but online abstract snippets alone cannot support full evidence extraction or a verified cost comparison.
- Search coverage remains exploratory and not systematic/exhaustive. The next stage is DOI-level deduplication and primary full-text eligibility screening, not counting all search hits as included studies.

## 10. Second evidence-audit batch — 29 October 2026

Follow-up primary-source audit completed for the CMC-functionalized magnetic GO TC paper and the GO–ZnAlNi LDH CIP paper; the dual-antibiotic DDMGO paper remains gated on full numerical table access.

- **SEM-018 / EV-SEM-007:** RSC full text now audited in detail. The 96.04% result is at C0=2 mg/L, 0.1 g/L sorbent dose, pH 5, 25°C and 25 min. Derived qe by mass balance is 19.208 mg/g. The highlighted 54.73 mg/g Langmuir q_m is the *linear* fit at 50°C; at 50°C the nonlinear fit returns 48.4744 mg/g, while the 25°C linear fit is 45.5581 mg/g. Methanol is used for regeneration; about 96% removal initially and 92% after 10 cycles are reported. The article gives extensive preparation detail but no quantified synthesis-cost inventory.
- **SEM-015 / EV-SEM-005:** the full article’s own adsorption-capacity equation enables an explicit consistency check. With C0=1 mg/L, sorbent 10 mg/L and 84.84% removal, implied qe is 84.84 mg/g, whereas the narrative gives 97.43 mg/g and abstract/Table 3 give 106.97/106.9 mg/g. Dose-series pairs do not reconcile either. The study remains eligible for qualitative/method discussion and the condition-specific removal result, but its capacity value is blocked from quantitative ranking until reconciled. Five-cycle removal falls from 80.4% to 27%.

The primary abstract is reliable for broad design/mechanism statements, not numeric performance. Secondary review figures (TC 294.12 mg/g; CIP 111.73 mg/g) have been logged as leads only; primary PDF access and exact conditions are still required.
